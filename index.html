Mission

Built to Cool the World.
Not Warm It.

"We exist to shift when energy is consumed, not just how much—turning every building into a node of grid stability."

Ice Battery was founded on a simple but powerful insight: the most effective way to decarbonise air conditioning is not to replace the compressor—but to move electricity consumption to moments when the grid is cleanest and cheapest. Using ice as a thermal battery, we decouple cooling demand from peak generation, permanently.

Carbon Neutral Cooling
Charge with overnight renewable surplus; discharge clean cold all day—zero marginal emissions.
Grid Resilience at Scale
Aggregated Ice Battery fleets act as dispatchable virtual power plants, flattening the duck curve.
Economic Accessibility

Flagship Product

ICE BATTERY™
Series III

The world's most advanced modular thermal energy storage system. Series III charges on off-peak electricity—typically overnight—freezing water inside an insulated tank. During peak hours, it discharges stored cold to offset or replace conventional chiller operation for up to eight hours, cutting electricity costs by up to 95% during the most expensive periods of the day.

The Charge / Discharge Cycle
Night — Off-Peak
Charging
Chiller freezes water to −22°C. Glycol loop removes heat. Ice block formed. Low tariff electricity used.
Day — Peak Hours
Discharging
Stored ice absorbs building heat passively. Compressor off for up to 8 hours. Peak costs slashed.
